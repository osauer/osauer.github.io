Canary Desk

Product sheet · October 2026

Rule-based protection for your portfolio, with an AI analyst on staff.

Canary Desk runs your risk rules on the live book: trailing stops, loss exits and a budget for option premium. A policy-based cash sweep puts idle money to work. While your Mac stays awake, it places the orders you have pre-authorised, even when you’re away, and drafts the rest for your approval. Before each open, its AI analyst briefs you on the markets and your book.

What the rules watch

Canary’s defaults, October 2026. You set every level.

  • Market regime. Canary’s sensors read volatility, credit, funding, currency, dealer gamma and breadth. As they signal stress, your limits tighten.
  • Stops and exits. Trailing stops, loss exits and profit trails, with one order per contract however many rules ask.
  • Margin and exposure. Excess liquidity is flagged below 30 % of NLV and again below 15 %, and new buys are warned. Net exposure bands narrow in stress.
  • Option premium budget. Long premium at risk, hedges excluded, is watched at 25 % of NLV, 15 % in stress. Above it the book is sell-only: new buys are warned, not blocked.
  • Cash sweep. Surplus cash into same-currency government bills, after commitments and liquidity reserves. Review the bill, maturity, price and fees. Off by default.
  • Ranked sell plan. At 35 % of NLV, Canary ranks what to sell by what each sale fixes and drafts the sales. Off by default.

Context and review

Written briefs. Before each open: markets, headlines on your holdings, how the book did. One model writes, a second edits.

An analyst you can question. It answers from a snapshot of your broker data; a second model checks each answer.

FX contribution. Currency effects on P&L in your base currency: Day, WTD, MTD and YTD, with daily statement evidence. Missing days stay visible.

Edge. What past trades delivered, confirmed by the broker, against leaving the position unchanged. History, not a forecast.

Performance. Time-weighted return from broker statements, net of transfers, with drawdown, against SPY.

Market data. Streaming quotes and day charts, an index ribbon, and a calendar of releases and earnings.

Around the clock

On a Mac mini. Run it day and night on a dedicated Mac mini. Your laptop connects over a private, encrypted link; the rules keep running when it sleeps.

Notifications. Orders waiting for you, risk-policy and mode changes, and data problems reach your Mac or laptop.

Rules that act. Pre-authorised stops, exits and budget cuts go out when their veto window ends. The sweep needs its own explicit authority; everything else waits for you.

Stops at the broker. A placed trailing stop rests at Interactive Brokers, for the day or until cancelled.

On your phone. Canary’s paired app shows alerts and can veto a pending order; away from home it needs the optional remote relay.

When a machine stops. If TWS logs out, rules stop and Desk flags it. If the Mac running Desk sleeps, nothing runs there; orders already at the broker keep working.

What it is not

  • No claim of edge. Results are measured, not promised; a stop can fill below its trigger.
  • Not a data vendor. No licensed news or consensus data.
  • One person, one account. No roles, no second approver, no other brokers, no Windows or Linux.
  • Single-leg options only. Multi-leg and adjusted options are shown, not traded.
  • Not a money mover. It places orders; it cannot withdraw or transfer funds.

Built for a working portfolio. Created by a senior technology leader with decades of experience in financial services and software, and used daily on his own portfolio. Runs on your Mac with Interactive Brokers and your OpenAI API key. Private access is arranged personally.

Full features

Canary Desk

Levels are Canary’s defaults as of October 2026, and you set every one.

Market context

  • Market regime: volatility, credit, funding, currency, dealer gamma and breadth.
  • Stress requires agreement across groups; missing data stays visible.
  • Ten market leaders against SPY and QQQ on price, breadth and activity.
  • Official economic releases and earnings checks for your holdings.
  • A live index ribbon, the VIX term structure and session calendars for five exchanges.

Portfolio and performance

  • Net liquidation, day P&L, margin headroom, cash, realised and unrealised P&L at a glance.
  • Time-weighted return and drawdown from broker statements, against SPY.
  • FX contribution: Day, WTD, MTD, YTD from daily statements; incomplete periods have no total.
  • Holdings with streaming quotes, day charts, 52-week ranges and option legs.
  • Contribution breakdown; allocation by underlying, industry and asset class.
  • Reconciliation status from broker statements, and an evidence panel per position.

Risk and rules

  • Canary’s risk assessment: exposure, margin, financing, option Greeks and stress findings.
  • A rulebook with limits you set, from issuer concentration to margin headroom.
  • Limits that tighten with the regime, and a sell-only state when premium or exposure runs over.
  • One risk-policy word on every view, and a drawdown brake from your own policy.

Cash sweep

Reserve cash→Same-currency bills→Back to cash
  • Commitments, liquidity needs and your buffer come first. No FX conversion.
  • Bills mature to cash. Liquidity shortfalls can prompt sales; an early sale can lose value.
  • Review price and fees; confirm or pre-authorise within caps and a veto window.
  • A decision history records every plan, with its reason and any checks that need attention.

Protection and orders

  • Rule proposals: stops, loss exits, profit trails, expiry closes, trims and budget cuts.
  • A ranked sell plan at the premium cap, showing what each sale fixes.
  • One order per contract, queued for the open or pre-authorised under your caps and veto window.
  • Every other order confirmed by passkey, Touch ID or Apple Watch.
  • A receipt for every attempt and a reference for every refusal.

AI analyst and briefs

  • Morning, pre-open and evening briefs, plus a Saturday edition.
  • One model writes, a second edits; a factual fallback if a run fails.
  • Reviews on the market’s clock, and in between when the book changes.
  • An analyst checked by a second model; reasons, sources and expiry.
  • Edge: a broker-confirmed review of what past trades delivered.
  • Research from official sources: Fed, BLS, BEA, ECB, SEC, Treasury and FRED.

Operations

  • Runs on a Mac mini; reach the console from your laptop.
  • Automatic or manual operation, with a recorded reason whenever the mode changes.
  • Menu-bar companion, status monitor, data gaps and follow-ups on a daily timeline.
  • Model spending with a hard ceiling; synthetic simulation mode.

Privacy and data

  • Runs on your Macs. No cloud service, no telemetry, nothing sent to the maker.
  • Orders to IBKR, public-data requests, and OpenAI calls with the book evidence each answer needs.
  • The broker connection stays in Canary; the AI has read-only tools.

What you need

  • A Mac that stays awake, with TWS or IB Gateway and Flex statements.
  • Your OpenAI API key.
  • For Touch ID approvals, macOS 26 or later with a Secure Enclave.
