Your portfolio, understood before the day begins.

Canary Desk is a daily investment workspace for one trader, a family office or a small desk: the overnight story, the book, its risks and a reviewed set of decisions, ready before the open. It stands on three foundations: Canary CLI for broker evidence and HyperServe for the server, both open source, and Torok, the private harness that survives a restart. Tower, an experiment, is the page that steers the agents building it all.

Built by Oliver Sauer, a software engineer who runs his own book on it. Canary CLI and HyperServe are open source; the private three are described here in enough depth to judge.
