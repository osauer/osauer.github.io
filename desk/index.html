Canary Desk

Your portfolio, understood before the day begins and protected while you’re away. It places the orders you have pre-authorised, within limits you set, and asks you about everything else.

Ask about a private preview→
01

Before the open

A written brief each morning: how your book did the day before, and what needs your attention today.

02

Every minute

Your risk rules check the book every minute, whether you’re watching or not. When one trips, it drafts the fix: a stop, an exit, a hedge or a budget cut.

03

While you’re away

Stops and loss exits you have pre-authorised go out on their own, so the book stays protected when you’re not at the screen.

04

Idle cash

Cash above your buffer buys short-dated government bills in the same currency, spread across maturities. When cash falls below the buffer, the nearest bill is sold. Matured bills return to cash.

Full features

What’s inside.

Market context

  • Market regimeEight readings in six groups, from volatility and credit to dealer gamma and breadth, read into one regime stage.
  • BreadthHow many S&P 500 names trade above their 50- and 200-day averages, with new highs and lows.
  • Live market dataIndices, VIX and your holdings through the day, each quote with its age.
  • CalendarEarnings and macro events ahead, flagged against what you hold.

Your book

  • PerformanceReturns from broker statements, measured against the S&P 500, with drawdown.
  • EdgeWhat your trades delivered compared with leaving the position alone.
  • ExposureAllocation by sector, stocks against options, and margin headroom.
  • ReconciliationFigures checked against broker statements. Gaps stay visible.

Protection

  • Risk rulebookYour limits, checked every minute.
  • Stress signalAn early warning when the regime turns.
  • Protection proposalsStops, exits, hedges and budget cuts, each with its reason.

Orders and cash

  • Pre-authorised rulesChosen protection acts on its own, within your limits.
  • Queue for the openConfirm a reduction before the open; it goes out once the market opens.
  • Cash sweepIdle cash into short-dated government bills, and back.

Every day

  • Morning briefWritten by one model, edited by another, with how yesterday went.
  • AI analystAsk about any position or risk. Answers cite the evidence.
  • DecisionsAssessments and proposals in one place, ready for your call.

Always on

  • Around the clockRuns day and night on a dedicated Mac mini, or any Mac that stays awake.
  • From your laptopYour laptop connects to it over a private, encrypted link.
  • NotificationsAlerts on your Mac and phone. Approve with Touch ID or Apple Watch.
