Private · investment agent

One book, watched all day, every decision written down.

Desk is the private agent that runs my Interactive Brokers book. It reads the broker through Canary, keeps its work in Torok and writes down what it thinks and why. It proposes. I decide. No order leaves without my passkey.

How it is built Message me on LinkedIn

What it does

Most of the day, nothing. Work starts when the calendar says so, when a rule sees something, or when I ask.

Runs on the market's clock

07:00 Berlin, an hour before each open, at the open, before the close, after it. Session times come from Canary, early closes included.

Evidence first

Account, positions, risk, regime, calendar, proposals and reconciliation are kept on their own clocks. A decision names its sources, a reason, what would prove it wrong and when it expires.

Ask Desk

A durable conversation with the book: interpret, gather evidence, analyse, review. A second model checks the answer against the same evidence. Missing evidence means no recommendation.

Trading is mine

Off by default. One single-leg DAY limit order at a time, drafted by Canary, previewed by the broker, confirmed with a passkey. No model can reach that handler.

A ceiling on spend

Every model call reserves against a monthly allowance before it goes out. Scheduled reviews get 48 hours of grace under a hard cap.

Dull to operate

One binary as a LaunchAgent. Health endpoints. Upgrades wait for running work. A notification only when a person is needed.

How it is built

Desk is mostly configuration. Canary owns the broker: session, prices, risk, calendars, proposals, reconciliation. Torok owns the work: inbox, schedules, bounded episodes, memory, recovery. HyperServe serves the console on loopback. Desk adds the instructions, nineteen read-only Canary tools, the observers and the shape of a decision. Models come from OpenAI. One analyses, another reviews and researches.

Screens

Five views, one masthead, one quote ribbon, Ask Desk on the right. Every figure carries its source and its age.

What it is not

  • Not a product. No download, no repository. It runs one book, mine.
  • No claim of edge. Correct operation and investment results are measured separately. Building it proves neither.
  • One machine. A user LaunchAgent, running while the Mac is awake and logged in.
  • Simulation in the pictures. Real screens look the same with real numbers, which is why they are not here.

Contact

Desk stays private, but the ideas travel: evidence contracts, a reviewer that is not the analyst, spend reserved before the request. If you are building something like it, write to me.
