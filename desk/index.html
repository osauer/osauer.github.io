Private · trading and risk management

A trading and risk desk with an AI analyst on staff.

Desk manages a brokerage book through the trading day: stops and hedges drafted by rule, a written brief before the open and after the close, AI-augmented research and decisions with dated evidence, and the portfolio numbers that matter, from exposure to time-weighted performance. It runs with Interactive Brokers today, through Canary; the broker layer is its own package, so other platforms can follow.

Personal work, on personal time. Desk is not connected to an employer, and nothing on this page is investment advice.

What it does

Rules first, then briefs, then AI where judgment is needed.

Stops and hedges by rule

Canary's protection rules draft trailing stops, exit watches and hedges per position, with no AI in the loop. You confirm each one, or pre-authorise the bucket and keep a veto window. Desk shows each one on the holding line and under Risk.

Daily briefs

A written brief at 08:30 Berlin, half an hour before the US open and at 18:00: positions, risk, the calendar, what changed, what needs you.

AI-augmented investing

Scheduled reviews at the open, before the close and after it. An analyst model proposes adds, holds, trims and exits with a reason, sources, what would prove it wrong and an expiry. A second model reviews before anything reaches you.

Ask Canary Desk

A durable conversation with the book: which positions need attention, exposure and regime, the next session. Evidence is gathered first. Missing evidence means no recommendation.

Portfolio management

Exposure, margin and Greeks, sector and asset mix, reconciliation against statements, and time-weighted performance against SPY, net of transfers.

On the market's clock

07:00 Berlin, an hour before each open, at the open, before the close, after it. Session times come from Canary, early closes included, so a holiday never runs a review.

How it is built

Desk is mostly configuration. Canary owns the broker: session, prices, risk, calendars, stops and hedges, reconciliation. Interactive Brokers is the broker behind it today, through Canary's own wire-protocol package; nothing above that layer knows the broker's name. Torok, the agent harness, owns the work: inbox, schedules, bounded episodes, memory, recovery. HyperServe serves the console on loopback. Desk adds the instructions, nineteen read-only Canary tools, the observers and the shape of a decision. Models come from OpenAI. One analyses, another reviews and researches.

What it is not

  • Not a product. No download, no repository. Licensed privately, if at all.
  • Not one broker's tool. Proven with Interactive Brokers today. The broker sits behind Canary's own package, so a second platform is a matter of building it, not of starting over.
  • No claim of edge. Correct operation and investment results are measured separately. Building it proves neither.
  • One machine. A user LaunchAgent, running while the Mac is awake and logged in.
  • Simulation in the pictures. Real screens look the same with real numbers, which is why they are not here.
  • Personal work. Built and run on personal time, not connected to an employer.

Contact

Desk stays private, but the ideas travel: rules before models, evidence contracts, a reviewer that is not the analyst. If you are building something like it, get in touch.
