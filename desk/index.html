01

Before the open

A written brief each morning covers how your book did yesterday and what needs your attention today. When an official economic release comes out, you get a prompt review of what it means for your portfolio.

02

Every minute

Your risk rules check the book even when you are not watching. A rule that trips drafts the fix, such as a stop, an exit or a budget cut. An unhedged book gets a flag, not a hedge.

03

While you’re away

Automatic protection is off until you explicitly authorise selected rules in Canary. Those rules can submit orders to reduce positions within your limits, after a notice period in which you can veto the order. That period is half an hour by default. The AI analyst cannot send orders.

04

Idle cash

A policy-based planner weighs same-currency government bills once commitments and liquidity reserves are covered. Off by default.

Opportunities · New with Canary v3.17

You choose the stocks.
Desk keeps watch.

Pick up to 20 stocks you know. While you work, Desk watches them for unusual volume followed by a price that holds or rises, and brings the setup to you with the chart behind it.

You decide what happens next. Choose the stock or a listed call, see the dated bid and ask beside your limit, and review the order before you confirm it.

An observation, not a forecast. Each entry needs your confirmation and must clear Canary’s risk checks. If one is held, Desk tells you why.

  1. 01 · WatchYour list, one setup

    A volume event that continues stays one setup as new bars arrive, so your review and your Ignore choice keep their place.

  2. 02 · ChooseThe price before the limit

    See a dated quote for the exact call, where your limit sits and the premium at risk as you type. Delayed or missing quotes are labelled.

  3. 03 · LearnA record worth keeping

    Mark each setup Worth a look or Skip. Quiet days count too, and entries placed through Canary get a timing review at two later checkpoints.

Timing reviews measure price before fees. They do not show realised profit.

accounted for.

A foreign holding can rise while its currency pulls your P&L down. Performance → FX shows the currency contribution in your account’s base currency for the day, week, month and year to date.

Daily contributions reconcile to completed broker statements. The reporting cutoff and any missing days stay visible, and an incomplete period shows no total. The figure is the currency effect on valuation, kept apart from realised Forex lot P&L.

Research · New with Canary v3.17

The price of borrowing,
and what your shares earn.

Compare borrowing costs across the stocks you hold or follow, and see whether a high rate persists across reporting dates or was a single reading.

Screen the wider US borrowing market by fee, price and liquidity, add FINRA’s reported short interest and days to cover, and add a name to your watchlist in one step.

Borrowing fees are a cost to borrowers, not income or a recommendation. Short interest comes from a dated, twice-monthly report and does not track live positions.

  1. 01 · Borrowing costsPersistent, or one reading?

    Dated rates for the stocks you name. Checking the same report again never counts as persistence.

  2. 02 · Research leadsBeyond your watchlist

    Each result shows price, daily move, volume, liquidity and year-to-date return. Missing data is marked and never counted as zero.

  3. 03 · Lending income · Released in Canary v3.16Your shares on loan

    Dated loans and their net fees from IBKR statements, linked from the holding. Fees stay separate from account P&L; participation stays with IBKR.

Plan cash with a reserveLiquidity, bill selection and order authority

Cash sweep

Plan the surplus.
Keep the reserve.

The cash-sweep planner considers short-dated government bills in the currency you hold, after commitments, liquidity needs and your policy buffer.

Review the bill, its maturity, price and fees, and the estimated gain after spread, fees and the cash interest you forgo. You choose which currency comes first. Settled cash must be current enough to include confirmed trades.

The sweep is off by default. Commissioning needs your calibrated policy and current settlement evidence, and sending an order is a separate authority you grant.

Bills can lose value if sold before maturity.

  1. 01 · Keep liquidCash for the book

    Commitments, liquidity needs and your buffer come first.

  2. 02 · Put the surplus to workSame-currency bills

    The plan picks a short-dated bill in the same currency. Any purchase still needs evidence and your authority.

  3. 03 · Replenish cashMaturity or a reviewed sale

    Bills mature back to cash; a liquidity shortfall can prompt a sale. Sale proceeds must settle.

Each proposal keeps its reason and decision history, and missing evidence holds the sweep.

Full features

What’s inside.

Market context

  • Market regimeEight readings in six groups, from volatility and credit to dealer gamma and breadth, feed one regime stage.
  • BreadthHow many S&P 500 names trade above their 50- and 200-day averages, with new highs and lows.
  • Live market dataIndices, VIX and your holdings through the day, each quote with its age.
  • CalendarEarnings and macro events ahead, flagged against what you hold. Official releases get a prompt impact review.
  • Borrowing costs · newDated borrow rates for the stocks you follow, and a wider US screen by fee, price and liquidity.
  • Short interest · newFINRA’s reported short positions and days to cover, with the report date always shown.

Your book

  • PerformanceReturns from broker statements, measured against SPY, with drawdown.
  • FX contributionThe currency effect on P&L for Day, WTD, MTD and YTD, backed by daily statements.
  • Lending incomeDated shares on loan and earned net fees, linked from each holding and readable by Desk’s analyst.
  • Account valueDeposits and withdrawals marked on the balance, kept apart from investment returns.
  • EdgeWhat your trades delivered compared with leaving the position alone, one step from Portfolio.
  • ExposureAllocation by sector, stocks against options, and margin headroom.
  • ReconciliationFigures checked against broker statements. Gaps stay visible.

Protection

  • Risk rulebookYour limits, checked every minute.
  • Stress signalAn early warning when the regime turns.
  • Protection proposalsStops, exits, trims and budget cuts, each with its reason.

Orders and cash

  • Pre-authorised rulesOff until you explicitly authorise selected protection rules in Canary. Each reduction waits through its veto window and must stay within your limits.
  • Queue for the openConfirm a reduction while the market is shut; it goes out shortly after the open.
  • Cash sweepA same-currency bill planner with your currency order and the estimated gain after costs. Off by default; commissioning needs calibrated reserves and current settlement evidence.

Every day

  • Morning briefHow yesterday went and what’s on today, written by one model and edited by another.
  • AI analystAsk about any position or risk. Answers cite their sources.
  • DecisionsAssessments and proposals in one place, ready for your call.
  • Opportunities · newUp to 20 stocks you choose, watched for unusual volume and a price that holds or rises. You see a dated quote before setting a limit, and eligible entries get a timing review. A setup is a hypothesis, and every order needs your confirmation.

Always on

  • Around the clockRuns day and night on a dedicated Mac mini, or any Mac that stays awake.
  • From your laptopReach it over a private, encrypted link.
  • NotificationsLocal macOS alerts report changes that need your attention. Canary’s paired app carries order alerts and vetoes. Confirm orders at the Mac with Touch ID or Apple Watch.

Your data and decisions

Analysis on your terms.

Desk runs on your Mac. OpenAI receives the portfolio evidence needed for analysis through your API or Codex connection. Broker requests go to Interactive Brokers, and market research uses external data sources. Desk sends no telemetry or portfolio data to its maker.

The AI analyst can read, explain and propose. An order reviewed in Desk needs your confirmation on a registered device. Separately, you can explicitly authorise selected protection rules in Canary to act within limits after a veto window. The cash sweep requires separate authorisation. Every order must pass Canary’s checks.

These controls do not guarantee an investment result. Prices can move before an order fills.
