Portfolio performance

The currency effect,
accounted for.

A foreign holding can rise while its currency pulls your P&L down. Performance → FX shows the currency contribution in your account’s base currency for the day, week, month and year to date.

FX contributionCurrency effect+Other P&LPrices, income and fees=Portfolio changeAfter external cash flows

Daily contributions reconcile to completed broker statements. The reporting cutoff and missing days stay visible; incomplete periods have no total. This measures the currency effect on valuation, separately from realised Forex lot P&L.

Cash sweep

Plan the surplus.
Keep the reserve.

The cash-sweep planner considers short-dated government bills in the currency you hold, after commitments, liquidity needs and your policy buffer.

Review the bill, maturity, price and fees. The feature is off by default; commissioning needs your calibrated policy and current settlement evidence. Sending the order is a separate authority you grant.

Bills can lose value if sold before maturity.

  1. 01 · Keep liquidCash for the book

    Commitments, liquidity needs and your buffer come first.

  2. 02 · Put the surplus to workSame-currency bills

    The plan resolves a short-dated bill in the same currency. Evidence and authority still gate any purchase.

  3. 03 · Replenish cashMaturity or a reviewed sale

    Bills mature back to cash; a liquidity shortfall can prompt a sale. Sale proceeds must settle.

Every proposal keeps its reason and decision history. Missing evidence holds the sweep.

Full features

What’s inside.

Market context

  • Market regimeEight readings in six groups, from volatility and credit to dealer gamma and breadth, read into one regime stage.
  • BreadthHow many S&P 500 names trade above their 50- and 200-day averages, with new highs and lows.
  • Live market dataIndices, VIX and your holdings through the day, each quote with its age.
  • CalendarEarnings and macro events ahead, flagged against what you hold.

Your book

  • PerformanceReturns from broker statements, measured against SPY, with drawdown.
  • FX contributionThe currency effect on P&L: Day, WTD, MTD and YTD, with daily statement evidence.
  • Lending observations · v3.16.0Canary’s dated shares-on-loan evidence and earned net-fee history, readable by Desk’s analyst.
  • EdgeWhat your trades delivered compared with leaving the position alone.
  • ExposureAllocation by sector, stocks against options, and margin headroom.
  • ReconciliationFigures checked against broker statements. Gaps stay visible.

Protection

  • Risk rulebookYour limits, checked every minute.
  • Stress signalAn early warning when the regime turns.
  • Protection proposalsStops, exits, trims and budget cuts, each with its reason.

Orders and cash

  • Pre-authorised rulesThe protection you choose acts on its own, within your limits.
  • Queue for the openConfirm a reduction while the market is shut; it goes out shortly after the open.
  • Cash sweepA same-currency bill planner, off by default. Commissioning needs calibrated reserves and current settlement evidence.

Every day

  • Morning briefHow yesterday went and what’s on today, written by one model and edited by another.
  • AI analystAsk about any position or risk. Answers cite the evidence.
  • DecisionsAssessments and proposals in one place, ready for your call.
  • Opportunities · in previewA saved watchlist, five-minute volume observations and the price response. A pattern match is a hypothesis; order review remains separate.

Always on

  • Around the clockRuns day and night on a dedicated Mac mini, or any Mac that stays awake.
  • From your laptopReach it over a private, encrypted link.
  • NotificationsAlerts on your Mac and phone. Approve with Touch ID or Apple Watch.
