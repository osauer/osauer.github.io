A daily rhythm for your portfolio.

From what happened overnight to what you will do about it, with the evidence one click away.

Begin with the full picture

A written brief early each morning and again before the US open: markets and macro, news on the names you hold, how the book performed, where the risk sits. A missing source is named, never papered over.

Know where you stand

Holdings, exposure, margin, financing and time-weighted performance against the index, on one screen. Every figure carries its source and its age, so a delayed quote is never mistaken for a live one.

Decisions you can defend

Every proposed add, trim, hold or exit comes with its reason, its sources, what would prove it wrong and an expiry. A second model reviews the analyst before anything reaches you.

Ask the analyst

Which positions need attention? What changed in the regime? What should tomorrow look like? The analyst gathers the evidence first, keeps the thread, and says so when it lacks what a recommendation would need.

On the market's clock

Reviews run before the open, at the open, before the close and after it, on confirmed session times, early closes and holidays included. Operations shows what ran, what is next and what needs you.

As much autonomy as you allow

Rules draft trailing stops, exits and hedges for every position. You confirm each with a tap, or pre-authorise a reduce-only bucket with a veto window. Everything else waits for you, on the paired companion or a passkey. Research never places a trade.

Discuss Canary Desk

Built around one source of broker truth.

One Go process on your Mac. Canary owns the broker: session, prices, risk, calendars, protection rules and reconciliation. Torok owns the work: schedules, memory, budgets and recovery after a restart. HyperServe serves the console on your own machine. Canary Desk adds the instructions, the review workflow and the workspace.

Models are kept at arm's length. One writes the brief, a second reviews it, a third does tool-using analysis, each on its own connection with its own budget. No model can reach the broker.

From the book to the next decision.

Five views, one set of evidence. Portfolio, Decisions, Risk, Market and Operations read the same clocks, and the analyst follows whatever you are looking at.

What to expect.

  • Licensed privately. Installed on your own Mac, terms discussed directly. No public download, price list or launch date.
  • Your machine, your data. The workspace runs on your Mac against your broker session and the model connections you configure.
  • Interactive Brokers today, used on a live book every trading day. The broker is one layer of the system, not its foundation, so a second platform is an addition.
  • No claim of edge. A completed review is a well-argued view, not a return. Nothing here is investment advice.
  • You decide. Research and reviews prepare decisions. Only you send an order: by confirming it, or by pre-authorising a reduce-only bucket you can veto.

See it on your own book.

If you run your own money, a family office or a small desk and want the morning brief, the reviews and the analyst in one place, get in touch. If you build systems like this, the architecture is worth a conversation too.
