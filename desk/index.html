A daily rhythm for your portfolio.

Move from what happened to what matters, with the evidence close at hand.

Begin with the full picture

A morning and pre-open briefing connects market and macro developments, news relevant to held positions, portfolio performance and risk. Source gaps stay visible.

Know where you stand

Holdings, exposure, margin, financing and performance share one workspace. Canary supplies the broker evidence and source clocks, so a delayed input keeps its context.

Review decisions with context

Proposed decisions carry a reason, sources, what would change the view and an expiry. An independent review checks the assessment before it is presented as reviewed.

Ask Canary Desk

Ask about the book, a position or the next session. The analyst gathers evidence and retains the conversation. Missing prerequisites are named and limit the recommendation.

Stay with the market day

Scheduled reviews follow confirmed market sessions, including holidays and early closes. Operations shows completed work, upcoming reviews and anything that needs the owner.

Keep the decision yours

Research and briefing do not authorize a trade. The optional order workflow requires a review of the exact terms and passkey confirmation, with Canary enforcing the order path.

Discuss Canary Desk

Built around one source of broker truth.

Canary owns market and broker observations, risk evidence, calendars, execution enforcement and reconciliation. Torok retains work, schedules and recovery. HyperServe serves the local console. Canary Desk brings the investment instructions, review workflow and workspace together.

The briefing writer and reviewer can run through Codex App Server using the owner's ChatGPT session, with no paid API fallback. Tool-using analysis uses the configured model API. Their execution and accounting remain separate.

From the book to the next decision.

Portfolio, Decisions, Risk, Market and Operations share the same evidence and source clocks. The analyst conversation follows the selected context.

Availability and operating boundaries.

  • Private development. The Canary Desk repository remains private. There is no public download, published price or launch date.
  • Local operation. The workspace runs on the owner's Mac and depends on its broker and configured model connections.
  • Evidence has limits. Missing, stale or unavailable sources remain visible. A completed review does not establish investment performance.
  • Your authority. AI research and scheduled reviews prepare decisions; they do not grant permission to submit orders.
